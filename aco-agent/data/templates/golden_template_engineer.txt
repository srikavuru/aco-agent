About Vertiv
Vertiv designs, builds and services critical infrastructure that enables vital applications for data centers, communication networks, and commercial and industrial facilities. Our portfolio of power, cooling and IT infrastructure solutions and services enables business continuity for our customers and protects people and the environment through projects and initiatives like the Vertiv Foundation and ESG commitments.

Role Summary
We are seeking a Senior Electrical Engineer to join our OneCore engineering team in Columbus, OH. In this role, you will own the design, validation, and technical documentation for AC power distribution equipment supporting hyperscale and enterprise data center deployments globally.

What You'll Do
- Lead electrical design of switchgear, PDUs, and UPS systems (Juice Box / SmartRun product lines)
- Own DFMEA and design validation protocols through FAT (Factory Acceptance Testing)
- Collaborate cross-functionally with Thermal (DLC/CDU), Controls (BMS), and Software teams
- Support technical sales and customer on-site commissioning globally
- Mentor junior engineers and review design documentation packages

What We're Looking For
Required:
- Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering (BSEE) or equivalent
- 5+ years of experience in power distribution, UPS, or switchgear design
- Proficiency in AutoCAD Electrical, SPICE simulation tools
- Strong understanding of NEC, UL 891, IEC 61439 standards

Preferred:
- PE license or actively pursuing
- Experience with hyperscale data center power architectures (N+1, 2N)
- Background at feeder companies: Johnson Controls, Carrier, Eaton power division (internal: exclude "Eaton" before publishing)

Compensation
Annual salary range: $105,000 – $130,000 USD. Final offer commensurate with experience, skills, and location. This range applies to Columbus, OH and equivalent cost-of-labor markets.

Benefits include: medical, dental, and vision insurance; 401(k) with company match; paid time off (PTO); paid parental leave; employee stock purchase plan; tuition reimbursement.
